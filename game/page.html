How to play
  • Tap one of your guti, then a glowing point to move one step along a line.
  • Jump over an enemy guti to the empty point straight behind it to capture it. Orange marks show jumps.
  • After a capture you may keep jumping with the same guti.
  • Win by capturing all 16, or by leaving your opponent with no move.
  • Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
