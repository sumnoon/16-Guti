You are the lead artist for a 3D mobile board game called Sholo Guti (the traditional Bangladeshi 16-piece game). We are creating ALL assets for a new environment, "Old Dhaka Rooftop Night." Everything must look like it belongs to ONE consistent world.

=== ART BIBLE (apply to every asset) ===
Setting: a flat cement rooftop in Old Dhaka (Shankhari Bazar / Lakshmi Bazar) during the Shakrain kite festival in mid-January, from dusk into night. The roof has a waist-high painted parapet, black plastic water tanks, a small stairwell room (chilekotha), potted marigolds and tulsi, a clothesline, and tangled electric wires overhead. A Sholo Guti board is drawn in white chalk on the rooftop floor with a jute mat on each side, a plate of murir moa and a lantern beside it. Hundreds of colourful paper kites (ghuri) fill the sky. On every neighbouring roof, groups of people fly kites with wooden spools (natai). Dense old buildings of different heights press together on all sides: peeling lime plaster, colour-washed walls, iron balcony grilles, lit windows, mosque domes and minarets. String lights and paper lanterns hang on every roof. At night, fire-breathers on nearby roofs blow bursts of flame, fireworks pop across the skyline and sky lanterns (fanush) drift up.
Style: photorealistic, lively, festive and crowded, full of energy. Grounded and real. No fantasy, no cartoon, no readable text, signs or logos.
Lighting (for concept and UI art only): dusk sky fading from violet to deep blue night, warm amber string lights, orange flashes from fire-breathers, coloured light from fireworks.
Color palette: dusk violet #5B4A8A, night blue #16213E, string-light amber #FFB84D, kite red #D7263D, kite green #1B998B, kite yellow #F4D35E, kite magenta #C2185B, old plaster #B9A48A, faded turquoise #5FA8A0, cement grey #8A8580.
Materials: old, weathered, layered, lived-in. Stained cement, peeling paint, rusty iron, sun-faded plastic. Nothing looks new or factory-perfect.
Scale: real-world sizes in metres.
The board: the Sholo Guti board in any generated image is decorative only and will be inaccurate. Never make it the focus. Show it small, partly covered by a mat or kites, at an angle, or out of frame. The developer draws the real board in the game.

=== TECHNICAL RULES ===
Textures: seamless tileable, square, top-down or flat front view, EVEN neutral lighting, NO shadows, NO perspective, highest resolution you can give. Facade textures: flat front view, straight-on, no perspective.
Panoramas: 360-degree equirectangular, 2:1 aspect ratio, eye level from a rooftop, empty foreground at the bottom, seamless left and right edges.
Effect sprite sheets: flat 2D frames on a pure black background, evenly spaced grid, no text, for additive blending.
3D models: .glb format (if not possible, .obj or .3mf). Real-world scale in metres, origin at bottom centre, textures embedded, single object, no background, no ground plane, game-ready low poly within the triangle budget given. Buildings are modular blocks with simple flat walls so many can be placed around the roof. Characters are static poses, no rig needed.
File names: use the names given below.
